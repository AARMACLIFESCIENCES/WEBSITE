Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026. This page applies to the Aarmac Life Sciences website and to enquiries you send us by email or telephone.

Who we are

Aarmac Life Sciences is a pharmaceutical company registered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. When this policy says "we" or "us", it means Aarmac Life Sciences at the address given at the foot of this page.

What we collect

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not sell anything online. We collect information in only two ways.

  • What you send us. If you write to us by email or call the number on this site, we receive your name, your contact details and whatever you choose to tell us in that message.
  • Standard server records. Like most websites, our host records the page requested, the date and time, the browser type and an IP address. These records are used to keep the site running and secure.

Why we use it

We use what you send us to answer your question, to discuss a product, a supply requirement or a role you have applied for, and to keep a record of that correspondence. We use server records to diagnose faults and to detect misuse. We do not use your details for automated decision-making or profiling.

Marketing

We do not send marketing email to people who have not asked for it, and we do not sell, rent or trade contact details to anyone.

Who else sees it

Your information is seen by the Aarmac Life Sciences staff who need it to answer you, and by the service providers who run our website, our email and our contact forms. Messages sent through the forms on our Careers & Contact page are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, a third-party form-relay service, which processes them only to pass them to us. We disclose information beyond that only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Correspondence is kept for as long as it is useful for the business relationship it belongs to, and then deleted. Job applications are kept for the recruitment cycle they relate to unless you ask us to keep them on file.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which is a third-party service and may record the request; no cookie is set by us for that.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to aarmaclifesciences26@gmail.com and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version appears on this page with a new "last updated" date.
