About us

About Aarmac Life Sciences

A pharmaceutical group in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, built from Aarmac Labs in 1997. Who we are, what we make and who writes this website.

What we are

Aarmac Life Sciences is a pharmaceutical company based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. We develop and supply branded formulations across twelve areas of medicine. Our products are supplied for the use of registered medical practitioners.

How the group grew

1997 — Aarmac Labs is founded. First-generation entrepreneurs Mr. Ramanathan and Mr. Chidambaram build the enterprise on a strong foundation of innovation.

Growth era — Rebrand to Aarmac Health Care. Strategic expansion broadens the portfolio, and the organization rebrands to reflect its evolving focus.

2021 — A new generation leads. Following the passing of Mr. Ramanathan, second-generation entrepreneur Mr. Anand assumes leadership alongside co-founder Mr. Chidambaram.

2026 — Aarmac Life Sciences launches. A pivotal milestone. The group broadens its scientific and clinical footprint under a dedicated division.

What we make

The range covers twelve therapeutic areas: antibiotics, pain management, ortho, obstetrics and gynaecology, cough and cold, diabetics, acidity management, skin care, multivitamins, iron and calcium, protein powder, and general practice and speciality products. Dosage forms include tablets, dispersible tablets, capsules, syrups, suspensions, sachets and topical preparations.

Every brand is listed on its range page with its name, its dosage form and its composition. See the full product range.

Who publishes this website

This website is published by the Aarmac Life Sciences team in Coimbatore. Product content is compiled from our own approved pack information and is checked by our team before it goes live. It is not written by an outside agency.

We do not publish patient-facing treatment advice, dosing guidance or clinical recommendations. That is the job of the prescriber and of the approved pack insert. How we decide what goes on this site is set out in our editorial policy.

Regulatory context

Medicines in India are regulated by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation. Standards for drugs are published by the Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission, which also runs the Pharmacovigilance Programme of India. Suspected adverse reactions to any of our products can be reported to us and to that programme.
