Obstetrics and gynaecology

Prescribing in obstetrics and gynaecology carries a second patient, and the margin for error narrows accordingly. This range covers the areas an O&G practice returns to: probiotics and vaginal health, prenatal and antenatal nutrition, inositol-based support in polycystic ovary syndrome, progestogen therapy, lactation support and the iron and vitamin cover that pregnancy demands. Tablets, capsules, sachets and syrups are supplied. Trimester and lactation status govern the choice; the approved pack insert carries the full contraindications.
