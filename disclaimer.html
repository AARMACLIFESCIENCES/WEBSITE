Medical Disclaimer

Last updated 21 September 2026. This page applies to the Aarmac Life Sciences website and to enquiries you send us by email or telephone.

For registered medical practitioners

The product information on this website is intended for registered medical practitioners in India only. Every product page on this site carries the line "For the use of Registered Medical Practitioner only". That line is not a formality — the medicines described here are prescribed, dispensed and monitored by qualified professionals.

This is not medical advice

Nothing on this website is a diagnosis, a prescription or a recommendation to take, stop or change a medicine. If you are a patient, speak to your doctor or pharmacist. Do not use this site to self-medicate, and do not stop a prescribed medicine because of something you read here.

In an emergency

If you think you or someone else is having a medical emergency or an adverse reaction to a medicine, contact a doctor or your nearest hospital immediately. Do not wait for a reply to an email sent to us.

Composition and prescribing information

Brand names, dosage forms and compositions shown here are for identification. Full prescribing information, including indications, contraindications, interactions, warnings and dosage, is in the approved pack insert supplied with the product. Where this site and the pack insert differ, the pack insert is correct.

Reporting a side effect

If you are a healthcare professional and you observe a suspected adverse reaction to one of our products, please write to aarmaclifesciences26@gmail.com with the brand name, the batch number and a description of what happened, or call +91 97894 88862. Suspected adverse reactions may also be reported to the Pharmacovigilance Programme of India.

No practitioner-patient relationship

Reading this website, or writing to us, does not create a doctor-patient relationship between you and Aarmac Life Sciences or any of its staff.
