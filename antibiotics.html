Antibiotics

Antibiotic choice in the community turns on the organism suspected, the site of infection and what the patient can reliably take. This range covers the broad-spectrum oral agents used for respiratory, urinary, skin and soft-tissue infections — amoxicillin-clavulanate combinations, oral cephalosporins, an oxazolidinone and a fluoroquinolone — in tablet, dispersible tablet and suspension forms for adults and children. Aarmac supplies these to registered medical practitioners only. We ask prescribers to keep antimicrobial stewardship in view: confirm the indication, complete the course, and de-escalate on culture where culture is available.

Every brand on this page is listed with its name, its dosage form and its composition, so that a prescriber can identify the pack without guesswork. Availability and pack sizes are confirmed by our team in Coimbatore before supply. Where the description here and the approved pack insert differ, the pack insert is correct.
