How we work

What we do, in plain terms.

We make medicines

Aarmac Life Sciences develops and supplies branded formulations. The group has been doing this since 1997. Today the range covers twelve areas of medicine, from antibiotics to skin care.

We supply to prescribers

Our products carry the line For the use of Registered Medical Practitioner only. That is not a formality. These medicines are chosen, prescribed and monitored by qualified people.

There is no call centre between you and us. Write to the address below or call the number. The team in Coimbatore handles the reply.

Common questions

Questions we are asked most.

Short answers to the things people write in about. If your question is not here, call us or use the contact form. A real person reads every message.

Who is this website for?

The product pages are written for registered medical practitioners in India. Doctors and pharmacists use them to check a brand name, a dosage form and a composition. If you are a patient, please speak to your own doctor. Do not use this site to choose a medicine for yourself.

What does Aarmac Life Sciences do?

We are a pharmaceutical company in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. The group started as Aarmac Labs in 1997. It later became Aarmac Health Care. In 2026 the group launched Aarmac Life Sciences. We make and supply branded formulations across twelve areas of medicine.

How many product ranges do you have?

Twelve. Each one covers a single area of medicine, such as antibiotics, pain, diabetes or skin care. Every range has its own page. On that page each brand is listed with its name, its dosage form and its composition.

Can I buy medicines on this website?

No. This website does not take orders and does not take payment. It is here so that a prescriber can identify a product. Supply is arranged directly with our team.

How do I check if a product is in stock?

Write to us or call us. Give the brand name and the pack size you need. Our team in Coimbatore will confirm what is available.

Where is the composition of each brand?

Each brand card on a range page shows the dosage form and the composition. Full prescribing information is in the pack insert supplied with the product. If the website and the pack insert do not agree, the pack insert is correct.

How do I report a side effect?

If you are a healthcare professional and you see a suspected reaction to one of our products, email us. Please include the brand name, the batch number and what happened. You can also report it to the Pharmacovigilance Programme of India.

Do you supply outside Tamil Nadu?

Write to us with your location and what you need. Our team will tell you what we can do.

How quickly do you reply?

Messages sent through our contact form reach our inbox directly. If your question is urgent, call us instead of writing.

Are you hiring?

Open roles are listed on our careers page. You can apply from that page and attach your CV.

Do you make the medicines yourselves?

Aarmac Life Sciences develops and supplies its own branded formulations. Each pack carries the Aarmac brand name and its approved prescribing information.

What is the difference between Aarmac Labs, Aarmac Health Care and Aarmac Life Sciences?

They are stages of the same group. Aarmac Labs was founded in 1997. The company later rebranded as Aarmac Health Care. In 2026 the group launched Aarmac Life Sciences as a dedicated division.

Who runs the company?

Aarmac Labs was founded by Mr. Ramanathan and Mr. Chidambaram. After Mr. Ramanathan passed away in 2021, Mr. Anand took up leadership alongside co-founder Mr. Chidambaram.

Can I get a printed product list?

Write to us and say which ranges you are interested in. Tell us how you would like to receive it.
