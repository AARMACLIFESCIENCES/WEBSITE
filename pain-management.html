Pain management

Analgesia is a balance between the pain relieved and the risk carried — gastric, renal, hepatic and, for opioid-adjacent agents, dependence. This range covers the ladder most Indian practices work through: paracetamol, NSAIDs including diclofenac and aceclofenac, serratiopeptidase enzyme combinations for post-operative swelling, muscle relaxant combinations for spasm, and topical preparations where systemic exposure is better avoided. Tablets, suspensions and topical forms are supplied. Duration matters as much as dose; the approved pack insert carries the full warnings.

Every brand on this page is listed with its name, its dosage form and its composition, so that a prescriber can identify the pack without guesswork. Availability and pack sizes are confirmed by our team in Coimbatore before supply. Where the description here and the approved pack insert differ, the pack insert is correct.
